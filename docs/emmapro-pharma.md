## 🧬 Medicine Batch Tracking & Traceability

EMMAPRO PHARMA supports **batch-oriented pharmaceutical inventory management**.

Batch tracking is important for pharmacies because the same medicine may be purchased at different times, from different suppliers, with different expiry dates or acquisition conditions.

The system supports medicine traceability through inventory and operational records, helping pharmacies maintain better control over individual stock batches.

This is especially valuable for:

- Medicine batch identification
- Single-batch tracking
- Batch-level stock control
- Expiry monitoring
- Stock traceability
